Partitioning, Bucketing & Pruning

Architectural Choices (Write-Time) vs. Performance Benefits (Read-Time)

1. WRITE-TIME (Producers)

"How we organize the physical storage on disk."

Partitioning

Physically organizing files into a folder structure.

df.write.partitionBy("date")
/date=2023-10-01/
/date=2023-10-02/

Bucketing

Pre-shuffling data into fixed-size files based on a hash.

df.write.bucketBy(100, "user_id")

Best for: Avoiding shuffles during large joins.

2. READ-TIME (Consumers)

"How the Engine leverages the organization."

Static Pruning

Spark skips folders based on your filter().

/date=2023-09-01/
/date=2023-10-01/ (Target)

Dynamic Pruning (DPP)

Spark skips folders based on the results of a join.

Engines find relevant keys from a small dimension table first, then only read matching folders in the large fact table.
