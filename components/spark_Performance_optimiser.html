Use Case

Joining a 10-year Sales table with a Date table filtered for just one month.

Benefit

The large table scan 'learns' from the small table at runtime and skips 99% of partitions.

When to use

Happens automatically in Spark 3.0+ for Broadcast Hash Joins.
